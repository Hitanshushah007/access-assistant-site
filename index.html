One employee.
Six jobs.

  1. Sells

    Understands what the customer wants, in any language and any spelling. Searches your real catalog, sends product photo cards with prices, builds the cart and closes the sale.

  2. Supports

    Order status, tracking links, returns and FAQs, answered instantly and 24/7 in the customer's own language.

  3. Collects payments

    Secure UPI and card payment links inside the chat. The customer never leaves WhatsApp, and your team is pinged the moment money lands, with items and address ready to dispatch.

  4. Sends GST invoices

    Your team asks in plain words and a proper GST invoice arrives in the chat as a PDF.

  5. Learns your business

    Every correction from your team becomes permanent knowledge. It gets better every week it runs.

  6. Knows when to step aside

    Anything it can't handle goes to the right person on your team on WhatsApp with a clean summary. When a human takes over the chat, Arch goes quiet.

Not a menu.
A conversation.

Most WhatsApp bots make customers press 1, 2 or 3. Arch talks the way your best staff member does: it remembers what was said, asks the right follow-up question and switches to Hindi when the customer does.

It never invents a price, a stock level or a delivery date. Those come only from your catalog and your systems. If it doesn't know, it says so and brings in a human.

Questions store owners ask

How much does it cost?

There's no one-size price. It depends on your message volume and what you need Arch to do, so we scope it after a free pilot on your own store. You see it working first.

Does it really speak Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. It replies in whatever the customer writes in, whether that's English, Hindi or Hinglish, spelling mistakes included.

What if a customer asks something it doesn't know?

It says so honestly and passes the chat to the right person on your team, on WhatsApp, with a short summary. It never guesses prices, stock or dates.

Can my team still reply themselves?

Yes. The moment someone from your team replies in a chat, Arch steps back so customers never get two answers.

Which stores does it work with?

Arch runs today on Access World's Shopify store. If your catalog lives somewhere else, tell us and we'll work out the setup together during the pilot conversation.

Can I try it right now?

Yes. Message Arch on WhatsApp and test it like a customer would. If you run a business, ask it for a sample GST invoice with your business name on it.
